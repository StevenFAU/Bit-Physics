## 1. Purpose

Ship an interactive, screen-space-rendered APIC water simulation whose moat is a **live, formally-proven correctness story no web fluid demo has**: an **angular-momentum-conservation readout** that stays flat for APIC and visibly decays for PIC (the rotating-disk signature), an **affine round-trip identity** (Prop 5.1) APIC satisfies bit-for-bit and PIC provably cannot, a **PIC/FLIP/APIC live comparison** of energy and noise, plus the inherited moat — pressure-Poisson divergence-residual heatmap (from smoke), transfer partition-of-unity + mass/momentum conservation (from MPM), deterministic fixed-point-atomic P2G, and a run-twice hash. The water the visitor pours is driven by *the exact APIC transfer whose conservation laws are proven and golden-tested*.

## 2. Why this sim (and positioning)

- **FACT.** APIC's signature properties are **formally proven exact equalities** (Jiang 2015 tech report Props 5.1/5.4/5.5), not tolerances — so they yield closed-form golden tests (backend § 7) that survive f32 and chaos. This is the strongest verification-visible moat of any sim in the portfolio.
- **FACT (maximal reuse).** PIC/FLIP = particle advection + the *identical* MAC/collocated pressure Poisson solve the `eulerian-smoke` reference already verifies, + the *identical* B-spline transfers `mpm-multimaterial` already golden-tests. The new sim inherits two verified subsystems and adds APIC's proven conservation on top (backend § 2 reuse table).
- **FACT (APIC is the GPU-determinism-friendly choice).** APIC's **lumped diagonal mass matrix** ⇒ per-node independent accumulate ⇒ a cheap, bit-reproducible fixed-point-atomic WGSL P2G scatter. FLIP's exact conservation needs a full (possibly singular) mass matrix — far harder to parallelize/bit-reproduce. So APIC is *both* the most verifiable *and* the most GPU-friendly (research finding 6).
- **FACT (positioning, v0.2 — full field survey, sources in § 2.2).** The famous "WebGPU water" demos (matsuoka-601's WebGPU-Ocean / WaterBall / Splash, holtsetio/flow) are **MLS-MPM with a weakly-compressible Tait EOS — none of them solve a pressure Poisson system at all**; they deliberately avoided the solver this demo verifies. The browser PIC/FLIP lineage (Ten Minute Physics #18, austinEng, dli/fluid, jeantimex/fluid) has **no APIC, no conservation instrumentation, and no correctness claim** — the closest peer, [jeantimex/fluid](https://github.com/jeantimex/fluid) (WebGPU SPH + PIC/FLIP, red-black Gauss-Seidel, velocity extrapolation, spray/foam/bubble whitewater), is the richest aesthetically but claims nothing. **No browser APIC demo exists anywhere** (verified by search, 2026-07-04). A verification-visible APIC demo with a live angular-momentum readout and a PIC/FLIP/APIC comparison is uncontested — and a *real projection* free-surface fluid in WebGPU is itself near-novel territory.

### 2.1 Lane & gating decision

Full posture: the backend reference is built + gated first (13-gate, `spec-ref.md`), then this frontend. Gate kind **`new_canonical`** (the SPH/MPM chaos-immune family), composed of:
- **Closed-form goldens (chaos-immune, unique to APIC):** GPU-evaluate the angular-momentum-conservation config → `L_after` vs `L_before` across P2G/G2P (Props 5.4/5.5) within the **declared f32-scale bound** (the identity is exact in rational arithmetic and dyadic-exact in f64 where the generator achieved it — backend § 7 FP-honesty rule; the browser shows the visitor's *measured* residual, never an asserted 0.0) vs the PIC negative control; the affine round-trip (Prop 5.1, **grid→particle→grid** — direction v0.2-corrected) reproduced to measured f32 ε; the transfer weights + `Dp⁻¹=4/dx²` vs the committed golden.
- **Transfer bit-identity:** fixed-point-integer-atomic P2G byte-identical to the O(n²)/lex-order oracle at moderate N (the boids/SPH brute≡sort analog).
- **Inherited structural:** partition-of-unity, mass/momentum conservation, divergence-free-post-projection (with the collocated O(dx²) floor).
- **Robust-observable match** to the committed dam-break capture (energy, momentum, bulk shape) — per-particle **not** gated (chaos + fixed-point≠f32-reference).
- **Run-twice determinism** (device-scoped bit-exact).

**Registration:** add `pic-flip` to `GATE_KIND` in `tools/productization/web-deploy/pipeline.py:51` (`"pic-flip": "new_canonical"`), `_gate_pic_flip` in `verify.py`, `[defaults.pic-flip]` in `tolerance.toml`.

### 2.2 Prior art & positioning (survey, 2026-07-04)

**Method lineage:** APIC (Jiang et al. 2015, [DOI 10.1145/2766996](https://doi.org/10.1145/2766996)) + angular-momentum analysis (Jiang et al. 2017 JCP 338, [PDF](https://math.ucdavis.edu/~jteran/papers/JST17.pdf)); graphics PIC/FLIP (Zhu & Bridson 2005; [Zhu MSc thesis](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~rbridson/docs/yzhu_msc.pdf)); Bridson *Fluid Simulation for Computer Graphics*; transfer unification via MLS-MPM (Hu et al. 2018).
**Code to mine (v0.2 — surveyed with numbers):**
- **[Ten Minute Physics #18](https://matthias-research.github.io/pages/tenMinutePhysics/18-flip.html)** ([code](https://github.com/matthias-research/pages/blob/master/tenMinutePhysics/18-flip.html), [slides](https://matthias-research.github.io/pages/tenMinutePhysics/18-flip.pdf)) — the canonical browser FLIP: MAC grid, Gauss-Seidel **+ SOR ω=1.9, 50 iterations**, flipRatio 0.9, **push-apart (2 iters) + density drift compensation (k=1, one-sided)** with a shipped `compensateDrift` toggle, solid-face `prevU/prevV` restore for the draggable ball. The two regularizers + BC-order recipe port to backend § 3.
- **[jeantimex/fluid](https://github.com/jeantimex/fluid)** — closest peer: WebGPU SPH + PIC/FLIP, **red-black Gauss-Seidel ("~2× faster than Jacobi")**, iterative velocity extrapolation into air, spray/foam/bubble whitewater triad, push/pull mouse, movable obstacles, tens of K particles at 60 fps.
- **[matsuoka-601's trio](https://github.com/matsuoka-601/webgpu-ocean)** (WebGPU-Ocean / [WaterBall](https://github.com/matsuoka-601/WaterBall) / [Splash](https://github.com/matsuoka-601/Splash)) — MLS-MPM (no Poisson), but the render + P2G patterns are the bar: **~100K particles on an iGPU (Ryzen 5825U) / ~300K dGPU / 1.6M in Splash's million branch**, fixed-point i32-atomic P2G, half-resolution SSFR, narrow-range filter (Truong & Yuksel 2018) in WGSL, velocity-stretched sprites, depth-gradient edge foam.
- **[austinEng/WebGL-PIC-FLIP-Fluid](https://github.com/austinEng/WebGL-PIC-FLIP-Fluid)** — 3D MAC, 5% PIC / 95% FLIP, PCG; the load-bearing datum is that it is **grid-bound, not particle-bound** (GTX 780: 60 fps @ 5K cells → 17 fps @ 100K cells): the pressure grid, not N, is the perf lever (§ 3.1). Don't rely on its "IPP preconditioner / two draw calls" detail (**refuted 1-2** in research).
- **[dli/fluid](https://github.com/dli/fluid)** (David Li, Google Experiments) — PIC/FLIP, ~100K+ particles, users **draw water blocks** pre-run (the INTERACT pattern § 4.1); [mobile-fluid-sim](https://github.com/shajidhasan/mobile-fluid-sim) (TMP FLIP + **gyro gravity**, HN-featured 2025) and mitxela's Fluid Pendant — proof device-tilt is the viral mobile interaction; [PavelDoGreat/WebGL-Fluid-Simulation](https://github.com/PavelDoGreat/WebGL-Fluid-Simulation) (~16K stars) — proof input-driven force splats are *the* engagement mechanic. Taichi FLIP/APIC examples, Bridson's `fluid3d`.

**White space (v0.2-verified):** no web fluid demo is *both* verification-visible *and* APIC (no browser APIC exists at all); none of the WebGPU-famous ones even solve a Poisson system; **none ships URL-shareable configs** (§ 4.1 — cheap, and for a verification demo a shared URL doubles as a repro link). The intersection — proven angular-momentum conservation + PIC/FLIP/APIC live comparison + real projection + screen-space water — is the moat.

## 3. Algorithm & engineering (frontend)

Per the backend § 3 (collocated-first APIC; MAC-staggered v2; **3D canonical** per the backend dimensionality decision — the SSFR reuse requires 3D particle positions). Frontend specifics:
- **Modes** `{PIC, FLIP, APIC}` as a live selector (not an α-blend as canonical — the APIC authors show pure APIC beats blends; research finding 1). APIC default. **(v0.2)** A *labeled, live-only* **FLIP-ratio slider** (0.9–0.99; Ten Minute Physics ships 0.9, austinEng 0.95) inside FLIP mode — the industry-standard blend as pedagogy, never gated.
- **P2G:** fixed-point-integer-atomic scatter (WGSL has no float atomics; APIC's lumped mass makes it cheap). **(v0.2 determinism note)** integer addition is associative ⇒ the atomic scatter is **order-independent — run-twice bit-identical on-device for free** (the mpm-multimaterial PR #6 proof); the fixed-point scale must bound |value|·count against i32 overflow (wraparound is deterministic but *wrong* — sized at the adaptive-N cap, measured). The sorted-gather alternative (CSR cell ranges, the `sph-water` pattern) is the documented fallback if overflow headroom gets tight. **f32** state; f16 only for non-gated visual buffers.
- **Projection (v0.2 — masked, new WGSL; live/canonical solver split):** the masked free-surface Poisson (cell labels, Dirichlet air `p=0`, zero-weight solid faces) is **new WGSL over the backend's new verified reference** — only the operator shapes come from smoke (backend § 1 correction). **Canonical/gated path:** fixed-iteration Jacobi exactly matching the reference (per-canonical pinned `n_iter`, backend § 6.3). **Live path:** red-black Gauss-Seidel + SOR ω≈1.9 (Ten Minute Physics runs 50 iterations; jeantimex measures RBGS ~2× Jacobi) + warm start — live only, labeled (warm start makes frames history-dependent; never in the gate). **Solver-depth honesty in-demo:** the pressure HUD shows `n_iter` vs tank depth in cells (Jacobi/GS propagate information ~1 cell/sweep — GPU Gems 3 ch. 30's "water sinks through the tank floor"); the hydrostatic self-test runs at its declared converged count.
- **Post-projection BC pass (v0.2):** solid-face velocity restore (`u·n = v_obstacle` — enables *moving* obstacles that throw water, the TMP draggable-ball mechanic) then air-cell velocity extrapolation (≥2 layers; without it splashes die sticky).
- **Advect:** RK2, CFL substeps. Marker particles for the free surface.
- **Regularizers (v0.2, backend § 3 step 6):** push-apart (2 iterations, via the cell hash) + one-sided density drift compensation (`−k(ρ−ρ₀)`, k=1) — ON by default (declared), with the **toggle surfaced as a teachable failure mode** (§ 4.3): off ⇒ clumping + the volume readout sinks. Müller calls the pair "necessary"; a demo without them visibly loses volume within seconds of a dam break.
- **Honest caveats surfaced in the demo (research caveats 1–2, v0.2-sharpened):** (a) angular-momentum conservation is exact *at the transfer level and dt=0*; end-to-end needs a symplectic/midpoint integrator (Euler/trapezoid break it — Jiang 2017 JCP, the correct source) — the reference uses symplectic Euler and the readout is labeled accordingly; (b) the 2015 proof is **collocated** — the collocated build claims the proven property directly; a MAC-staggered v2 would adopt the **Ding, Shinar & Schroeder 2020** APIC-MAC formulation (v0.1's "Jiang 2017/2020" was a misattribution); (c) **APIC dissipates energy even at Δt=0 where FLIP does not** (Ding 2020, abstract — sharper than v0.1's "comparable to FLIP" and stated alongside it). The collocated **checkerboard null-space** is shown honestly (shared with smoke).

### 3.1 Performance budget (v0.2 — measured-then-declared, the `sph-water` § 3.6 pattern)

- **The lever is the grid, not N (austinEng's measured curve: 60→17 fps from 5K→100K *cells* on a GTX 780):** unlike the EOS-based MLS-MPM demos (2 substeps, no solve), this demo pays per-cell pressure iterations. Live 3D grid **64³ target / 48³ floor** (adaptive), pressure at 40–60 RBGS iterations; grid arrays are the residency to watch, not particle buffers.
- **Particle targets (initial, to be measured at Step 2's checkpoint):** 100–150K dGPU / 30–60K iGPU with full SSFR — bracketed by in-repo measurements (sph-water solver 2.4/3.2/6.9 ms at 8/30/60K; mpm 120K×18sub = 13.9 ms) and the field bar (WebGPU-Ocean ~100K on a Ryzen 5825U iGPU, but with *no* pressure solve — do not promise its numbers).
- **Adaptive-N probe** (the sph-water pattern) sets N and grid together; **single substep at CFL** via RK2 where dt allows; workgroup 64; P2G fixed-point scale sized to the N cap.
- **Render is ~fixed-cost:** half-resolution SSFR (WaterBall's single biggest lever) + narrow-range filter ≈ 2–4 ms at half-res 1080p on iGPU-class — budgeted separately from sim; the free foam stack (§ 4.4) rides existing passes.
- **No silent caps:** when adaptive-N or grid-floor engages, the HUD says so (measured-then-declared, architecture § 2.6).

## 4. Scope — four layers

### 4.1 INTERACT
- **Pour / emit** (click-drag stream), **splash/push** (impulse force splat — the PavelDoGreat-proven engagement mechanic), **draw water blocks** (paint particles pre-run, the dli/fluid pattern), **drawable obstacles** + a **draggable moving obstacle** that throws water (velocity BC via the § 3 solid-face restore — the TMP red-ball mechanic), **tilt gravity** (slider on desktop, **device-orientation on mobile** — the proven viral mobile interaction: mobile-fluid-sim, Fluid Pendant), particle-count (adaptive cap), gravity, dt (live only; canonical pinned).
- **The PIC / FLIP / APIC selector — the signature control:** switch modes live and watch the energy plot + the surface behavior change (PIC smears/dissipates, FLIP gets noisy/jittery, APIC stays crisp+stable). Inside FLIP mode, the labeled live-only **FLIP-ratio slider** (§ 3). **(v0.2 stretch, adaptive-N permitting) split-screen A/B race:** the same scene stepped by two modes side-by-side at reduced N — the tradeoff as one glance; feasibility measured at Step 2, not promised.
- **Template gallery (v0.2 — named data-spine cards with caption + citation, the `sph-water` § 4.1 convention; each a params-preset, several ARE verification runs):** **Dam break** (the gated canonical — with live gate re-run readout), **Double dam** (two columns collide), **Rotating disk** (the angular-momentum showcase — the flagship PROVE preset), **Still pool** (the null test, regularizers ON — must stay still), **Hydrostatic column** (p=ρgh probe readout at the declared converged `n_iter`), **Waterfall / faucet**, **Sloshing tank** (tilt-driven), **Ball drop** (obstacle splash), **"Watch it sink"** (drift compensation OFF — the teachable failure, volume readout sinking, Müller-style), **Mode race** (the A/B split if it survives measurement).
- **URL-shareable scene state (v0.2):** preset + mode + seed + toggles round-trip through the URL (the mpm-multimaterial pattern; **no surveyed fluid demo ships this** — and for a verification demo a shared URL doubles as a repro link).
- **Grid-field overlay toggles** (velocity / pressure / divergence / cell labels {solid, fluid, air} / the grid) — aesthetic *and* verification instruments.

### 4.2 EXPLAIN
- The hybrid pipeline (P2G → grid solve/project → G2P → advect), the **APIC affine transfer** (`Cₚ=BₚDp⁻¹`, `Dp⁻¹=4/dx²`), and the PIC/FLIP/APIC difference (full velocity vs increment vs affine) — each next to the committed WGSL line and the reference it ports (`apic.py` anchors). Snippet anchors extracted at build time, self-healing.
- **Why APIC:** the PIC-dissipative / FLIP-noisy / APIC-stable tradeoff, cited to Jiang 2015/2017; the lumped-vs-full mass matrix and why it matters for GPU determinism.
- **The honesty tie-ins:** the integrator caveat, the collocated-vs-MAC caveat, the checkerboard null-space (shared with smoke), and the "APIC dissipation is *comparable to* FLIP, not zero — stable middle ground, not universally best" qualifier (research finding 7).

### 4.3 PROVE (the moat — proven, closed-form, largely inherited)
- **Live angular-momentum conservation readout — flagship.** Run the rotating-disk config (regularizers OFF — inert there anyway, backend § 3); plot total angular momentum vs step for the selected mode: **APIC flat** (conserved, Props 5.4/5.5, to the symplectic integrator's caveat — labeled), **PIC decaying** (the loss that causes its dissipation). Toggle modes and watch the curves diverge. Proven physics, live.
- **Affine round-trip identity (Prop 5.1 — direction v0.2-corrected).** At dt=0, take an affine **grid** field `vᵢ = v + Cxᵢ` **grid → particles (G2P) → grid (P2G)**; APIC reproduces it, PIC does not (paired negative control). Closed-form, chaos-immune. **Residual honesty (the `neural-ca` lesson):** the in-page **TS f64 mirror** (the `sph-water` pattern) shows ~1e-15; the WGSL f32 path shows the **visitor's measured** residual against the declared f32-scale bound — never an asserted 0.0 (fixed-point P2G ≠ f64, and the identity is bit-exact only in the generator's dyadic construction, backend § 7).
- **Volume / water-level readout + the drift toggle (v0.2 — a failure mode peers hide).** Live total-fluid-volume trace; with drift compensation ON it holds flat, OFF it sinks secularly (the "Watch it sink" template) — regularizers shown *as* regularizers, declared, not smuggled.
- **Golden transfer weights** — GPU-evaluate `wᵢₚ` + `Dp⁻¹` vs the committed `apic-transfer-weights.json` (FP-equivalent to the MPM B-spline golden).
- **PIC/FLIP/APIC energy comparison** — total kinetic energy vs step across modes (PIC decays, FLIP noisy, APIC stable) — the tradeoff, measured.
- **Divergence-residual heatmap** (reused from smoke) — `‖∇·u‖` after projection, with the honest collocated O(dx²) floor.
- **Transfer bit-identity** — fixed-point-atomic P2G SHA-256 == lex/oracle at moderate N.
- **Self-tests:** hydrostatic column (`p=ρgh` at the **declared converged `n_iter`** — the HUD shows iterations vs tank depth in cells, backend § 6.3 solver-depth honesty), still-pool-stays-still (max |v| floor; regularizers ON — inertness is itself an invariant), run-twice determinism hash, live gate re-run against the committed capture on the visitor's GPU (robust observables).
- **Verification card** bound to committed files (via § 5): gate kind `new_canonical`; golden shas; capture provenance; reference determinism `bit-exact-same-hw`, browser `epsilon` device-scoped.

### 4.4 RENDER
- **Screen-space water** (reuse the SPH pipeline as shipped in its v0.2 spec: half-res depth → thickness → **narrow-range filter** (Truong & Yuksel 2018 — the sph-water pipeline already promoted it over bilateral; Splash has the WGSL reference) → normals → Fresnel/refraction + Beer-Lambert) — pure aesthetics. Marker particles as the surface source; **velocity-stretched sprites** (WaterBall's cheap anisotropy) on energetic particles.
- **Free foam stack (v0.2 — near-zero cost, the sph-water § 3.5 stack, shared module):** depth-gradient edge whitening (WaterBall: maxΔz > 1.5·d), thin-thickness whitening, and a |Δv| foam channel splatted into the existing thickness pass — dam-break crests read white without any new solver.
- **Whitewater v2 (deferred with a concrete recipe):** Ihmsen et al. 2012 secondary spray/foam/bubbles — post-process, one-way coupled, no diffuse-diffuse forces; classification by **fluid-neighbor count** (<6 spray, >20 bubbles, else foam; the paper explicitly *declines* Weber number); ~10–30% overhead in the paper's scenes; [jeantimex/fluid](https://github.com/jeantimex/fluid) proves the triad is feasible in browser WebGPU. The single highest-leverage visual upgrade for dam-break scenes (Storm HydroFX shipped it as its first post-launch feature) — v2 because it rides the same marker data and touches nothing gated.
- **Grid-field visualization** — velocity glyphs, pressure/divergence heatmaps, cell labels, the grid itself (doubles as PROVE overlay).
- **Debug/honesty views** — raw markers colored by mode/speed/affine-magnitude (`‖Cₚ‖` — literally *watching the APIC matrix work*). hiDPI; poster + motion loop.

## 7. Governance & constraints

- **Full posture, real gate from day one** — backend built + gated first; frontend `new_canonical`. Not experimental-labeled.
- **HARD BOUNDARY:** the frontend is additive; no edits to the verified reference/golden/capture once landed. New materials/variants (MAC, viscosity, PolyPIC) are separate backend specs.
- **Cross-device bit-exactness NOT claimed** — device-scoped bit-exact + cross-device distributional; the integrator + collocated + fixed-point-vs-f32-reference caveats stated in-demo.
- **Precision:** f32 state; fixed-point integer atomics for P2G (determinism + bit-identity depend on it).
- **Frame-indexed animation only**; **standalone-serve** (bundle-local data, no `../../`); **no new heavy deps**; colormap + screen-space-render modules consumed, not edited.
- **Deploy:** adds a landing card + `packages/pic-flip/web/` bundle; publishing operator-dispatched (`confirm_deploy=true`; no write token here).
