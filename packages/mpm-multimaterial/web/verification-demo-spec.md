## 1. Purpose

Deliver on the `mpm-multimaterial` name: a **snow + sand + elastic-jelly + water showcase in a single grid solve** — MPM's unique superpower, uncontested on the open web — built on top of the verified neo-Hookean MLS-MPM core, and rendered so each material *reads* as itself (snow packs, sand piles and avalanches, jelly wobbles, water splashes). The moat versus every pretty-but-uncertified particle demo is a PROVE layer that *grows* with the materials: a **live golden-value B-spline shape-function readout** (matched to the committed table at ~1 ULP), **partition-of-unity** and **mass-conservation P2G/G2P** plots, a **deterministic-P2G bit-identity proof** (fixed-point integer atomics make the scatter order-independent), and **new per-material invariants** — snow's singular-value-clamp bounds, sand's volume-preserving Drucker-Prager return map (`tr(Hp)=tr(εp)`). Adding materials *extends* verification-visible coverage rather than diluting it.

## 2. Why this sim (and positioning)

- **FACT.** The core is already verified: golden B-spline shape-function table (abs 1e-15), `partition_of_unity_b_spline` and `mass_conservation_p2g_g2p` invariants, and a `bit-exact-same-hw` determinism gate (lex-order P2G/G2P, `sim.py`). The 88-line MLS-MPM reference's quadratic weights match this golden table to **2.22e-16** with partition-of-unity summing to exactly 1 (research § finding 2).
- **FACT (multi-material is the differentiator).** MPM supports multi-material coupling in one background-grid solve "solely by giving particles different material properties or constitutive models" — no separate coupling machinery (Jiang et al. 2016 SIGGRAPH course, [DOI 10.1145/2897826.2927348](https://doi.org/10.1145/2897826.2927348); quote verified verbatim in the course notes). EA's **PB-MPM** (official SIGGRAPH 2024 repo) runs four materials (liquid / elastic / sand / visco — **no snow**) in one **2D** WGSL solver. The "multiphase needs two separate grids" alternative was **REFUTED 0-3** in the research.
- **FACT (materials are additive AND extend verification — but they are NEW code).** Snow = SVD singular-value clamp + exp hardening (Stomakhin 2013); sand = StVK-on-Hencky + closed-form Drucker-Prager return map (Klár 2016); water = track only `J=det(F)` with a weakly-compressible ψ(J) (Tampubolon et al. 2017). **Lineage honesty (v0.2):** the hardening + SVD-clamp recipe is proven in Hu's upstream `mls-mpm88.cpp` (2D, **fixed-corotated**, snow plasticity built in), but the repo's *verified* reference `packages/mpm-multimaterial/mpm_multimaterial/reference/mls_mpm.py` is **neo-Hookean-only, no plasticity** (`compute_particle_stresses` hardcodes `material_id == 0`; snow is Phase-2+ deferred in `docs/sim-specs/hybrid-pg/mpm-multimaterial/algebraic.md`). So snow/sand/water are genuinely *reference-less* additions whose per-material invariants (§ 3.2) are their **only** verification — which is exactly why each must ship with one. Each adds a *new testable invariant* — the showcase grows the moat.
- **FACT (MPM is the high-N method).** Grid-transfer, no neighbor search — the reason WebGPU-Ocean switched from SPH (~30K) to MLS-MPM (~100K iGPU / ~300K mid-range dGPU), and the reason its successor **Splash** reaches **~1.6M water particles** in-browser on a desktop dGPU. Multi-material 3D at 200K was demonstrated in-browser as early as 2021 (WebGPUMPM, fluid+jello+snow, unoptimized). MPM is the right home for the high-particle-count spectacle SPH could not reach. *(Multi-material branching + SVD return-maps cost per-particle FLOPs and a fatter particle struct — not an order of magnitude; targets in § 3.3, measure at build.)*
- **FACT (first GPU MPM in this repo).** `mpm-multimaterial-stack-d` is Taichi-**CPU**, `-stack-e` is Warp-**CPU** — the web build is the repo's first GPU execution of this sim, which is why the browser-side determinism proof (§ 3.3) carries real new information rather than replaying a CPU result.

### 2.1 Lane & gating decision

Authors a new WGSL kernel *and* new constitutive models. Like SPH (and unlike smoke), the canonical is high-dimensional and chaos-prone, and — critically — the GPU's **fixed-point P2G is not bit-identical to the f32 lex-order CPU reference** (§ 3.3). So the gate is **`new_canonical`**, composed of chaos-immune + device-scoped criteria:

- **Closed-form exactness (chaos-immune, unchanged by the port):** the browser evaluates the quadratic B-spline weights on the GPU and matches the committed `mls-mpm-shape-functions.json` to golden tolerance (1e-15-class); partition-of-unity ∑w=1.
- **Mass-conservation P2G/G2P:** total mass and momentum transferred particle→grid→particle conserved to tolerance (the `mass_conservation_p2g_g2p` invariant on the GPU); the fixed-point encoding must be shown *not* to leak mass (itself a verifiable check).
- **Deterministic-P2G bit-identity + run-twice:** fixed-point integer-atomic P2G is order-independent → run-twice byte-identical on the same device (device-scoped bit-exact).
- **New per-material invariants:** snow elastic singular values stay in [1−θc, 1+θs]; sand Drucker-Prager preserves volume `tr(Hp)=tr(εp)` and separates stress-free at the cone tip.
- **Robust-observable match to the captures (NOT per-particle):** the small **5K/16³/50-step** capture on tight observables (short horizon limits f32↔f64 drift); the **1M/128³/500-step** capture on chaos-tolerant aggregates (center-of-mass trajectory, total KE, bulk height/volume, per-material mass fractions). Per-particle positions are explicitly NOT gated.

**No experimental label** — every criterion is real and committed. **Registration cost:** add `mpm-multimaterial` to `GATE_KIND` in `tools/productization/web-deploy/pipeline.py:53-67` (`"mpm-multimaterial": "new_canonical"`), add `_gate_mpm_multimaterial` to `verify.py`. **Tolerances (corrected v0.2):** the web gates cite the existing category rows verbatim — sph-water uses `[defaults.sph]`, smoke uses `[defaults.smoke]` — so this gate cites **`[defaults.mpm]`** (`tools/testkit/equivalence/tolerance.toml:30`, relative 1e-4) for capture-observable comparisons; do NOT add a `[defaults.mpm-multimaterial]` section, and note `[overrides.mpm-multimaterial]` (`tools/testkit/equivalence/tolerance.toml:141`) is the *cross-stack* row, not the web gate's. Web-only observable tolerances are measured-then-declared in the gate itself per the sph-water precedent.

### 2.2 Prior art & positioning (web + literature survey, 2026-07-03)

**The method (what we port):**
- **Hu et al. 2018**, MLS-MPM (SIGGRAPH, [DOI 10.1145/3197517.3201293](https://doi.org/10.1145/3197517.3201293)) — fuses APIC affine momentum + moving-least-squares stress into **one** P2G contribution (`Qp = ΔtVp⁰Mp⁻¹P(Fp)Fpᵀ + mpCp`, the 88-line `affine = stress + mass·C`); eliminates weight-gradient computation; merges APIC's two matrix-vector products into one (Gao et al. 2018, [GPU-MPM PDF](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~sifakis/papers/GPU_MPM.pdf)). The GPU-friendly transfer. *(Corrected v0.2 — the earlier research pass wrongly refuted the 2×: the paper's own abstract claims 2×, scoped in the body to **explicit** integration — which is exactly what we port, so the claim applies here; less significant for implicit.)*
- **The MIT 88-line reference** ([taichi_mpm, Hu](https://github.com/yuanming-hu/taichi_mpm/blob/master/mls-mpm88-explained.cpp)) — C++ *and* JavaScript (README-confirmed; Roberto Toro's mls-mpm.js, March 2019); **2D, fixed-corotated + snow plasticity** (`e = exp(hardening·(1−Jp))` scaling both Lamé parameters; SVD clamp `[1−2.5e-2, 1+7.5e-3]` — verified in source). Quadratic B-spline weights matching our golden table to ~1 ULP. The *transfer* math to port — but note the constitutive lineage split: our verified reference is 3D neo-Hookean (log J), not fixed-corotated (§ 2 lineage-honesty FACT). Browser JS port: [r03ert0/mls-mpm.js](https://github.com/r03ert0/mls-mpm.js). Practical APIC operationalization: [nialltl's MPM guide](https://nialltl.neocities.org/articles/mpm_guide) (quadratic (Dp)⁻¹ = 4 at Δx=1; course notes give Dp = ¼Δx²I quadratic, ⅓Δx²I cubic).
- **Jiang et al. 2016**, "The Material Point Method for Simulating Continuum Materials" (SIGGRAPH course, [DOI 10.1145/2897826.2927348](https://doi.org/10.1145/2897826.2927348)) — the canonical reference; C1-continuity (quadratic/cubic B-spline) to avoid cell-crossing instability; APIC + the constant-Dp simplification.

**The materials (the showcase):**
- **Stomakhin et al. 2013**, "A Material Point Method for Snow Simulation" (the Disney *Frozen* method, ACM TOG 32(4):102, [DOI 10.1145/2461912.2461948](https://doi.org/10.1145/2461912.2461948)) — F = Fᴱ·Fᴾ, fixed-corotated energy, exp hardening `µ=µ₀e^{ξ(1−Jᴾ)}`, SVD clamp to [1−θc, 1+θs] (θc=2.5e-2, θs=7.5e-3).
- **Klár et al. 2016**, "Drucker-Prager Elastoplasticity for Sand Animation" (ACM TOG 35(4):103, [DOI 10.1145/2897824.2925906](https://doi.org/10.1145/2897824.2925906)) — StVK on Hencky (log) strain + closed-form return map in principal-stretch space (3 cases), non-associative flow preserving volume.
- **Fluid (corrected v0.2):** weakly-compressible via `J=det(F)` tracking only, pressure from a ψ(J) energy — the correct citations are **Tampubolon et al. 2017** (water-sand mixtures, ACM TOG 36(4), [DOI 10.1145/3072959.3073651](https://doi.org/10.1145/3072959.3073651): ψʷ(Jʷ) with Jʷ evolved as a scalar) and nialltl's Tait-EOS fluid. EA PB-MPM's `MaterialLiquid` is J-only *in spirit* but enforces incompressibility via its position-based constraint, **not** an EOS — cite it for engineering, not for this material model.

**Reference code to mine (expanded v0.2 — all source-verified):**
- **[electronicarts/pbmpm](https://github.com/electronicarts/pbmpm)** (EA SEED, SIGGRAPH 2024; **BSD 3-Clause**; paper: Lewin, [DOI 10.1145/3641233.3664323](https://doi.org/10.1145/3641233.3664323)) — the engineering goldmine: WGSL **fixed-point integer atomics** (`encodeFixedPoint(f)=i32(f·M)`, M uniform-supplied, default **1e7**), the **bukkit** pipeline (6×6-cell tiles + 1-cell halo; per-step counting-sort `bukkitCount → bukkitInsert → bukkitAllocate`; **indirect dispatch** — one 64-thread workgroup per 64 particles per non-empty bukkit, empty space costs zero), and a **fused `g2p2g.wgsl`** that stages the grid tile in `var<workgroup>` atomic i32 arrays (src + scatter-dst ping-pong) and flushes once. Four-material branch structure (liquid/elastic/sand/visco). *Reuse its engineering, NOT its integrator* — PB-MPM is Position-Based (constraint-solve, Jacobi) and 2D, not the explicit MLS-MPM our verified core uses (§ 9).
- **[matsuoka-601/webgpu-ocean](https://github.com/matsuoka-601/webgpu-ocean)** — MLS-MPM in WebGPU at ~100K iGPU / ~300K dGPU + screen-space rendering; fixed-point multiplier **1e7** as a WGSL `override` constant; notably **plain global i32 atomics, no sort, no tiling** — proof the simple path already scales.
- **[matsuoka-601/Splash](https://github.com/matsuoka-601/Splash)** (2025, MIT) — the in-browser high-water mark: **~1.6M water particles** on a desktop dGPU. Levers to steal: **1 substep/frame** (Tait γ 5→1 + stiffness bump), a **`p2gDensity` pass building a density grid reused for ray-marched shadows**, and the **narrow-range filter** (Truong & Yuksel 2018) replacing the bilateral for visibly cleaner water silhouettes.
- **[chetan-parthiban/WebGPUMPM](https://github.com/chetan-parthiban/WebGPUMPM)** (UPenn CIS565, ~2021) — the closest 3D multi-material precedent: **fluid + jello + snow coupled, 200K particles real-time**, explicit MPM… rendered as raw GL points, zero verification claims. Both the existence proof for our particle budget and the foil for our polish + proof positioning.
- **[holtsetio/flow](https://github.com/holtsetio/flow)** (three.js WebGPURenderer/TSL, MIT) — MLS-MPM at 32K default / **131K max with instanced rounded-box meshes, shadows and bloom** — proof that instanced-*mesh* particles (not just sprites) are viable at ~130K in-browser.
- **[Wang et al. 2020 "claymore"](https://github.com/penn-graphics-research/claymore)** (multi-GPU MPM, [DOI 10.1145/3386569.3392442](https://doi.org/10.1145/3386569.3392442)) — **G2P2G kernel fusion**: transfer is ~80% of a substep, fusing G2P+P2G halves particle traffic and delivered **~2×**; PB-MPM's bukkit `g2p2g.wgsl` is exactly this pattern already expressed in WGSL. The optimization north star for § 3.3.
- **[Gao et al. 2018, GPU-MPM](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~sifakis/papers/GPU_MPM.pdf)** — the warp-reduction P2G alternative (CUDA ballot/shfl; see § 9 for the corrected WGSL-subgroups status and why it stays off the gated path).

**White space (positioning, sharpened v0.2).** Multi-material 3D browser MPM *exists* (WebGPUMPM 2021: fluid+jello+snow at 200K — raw points, no sand, no correctness claims, Chrome-Canary-era) and multi-material WGSL engineering exists (PB-MPM — 2D, no snow, no correctness claims); polished single-material water reaches 1.6M (Splash). What does **not** exist anywhere surveyed: a demo that is *simultaneously* multi-material-in-one-solve, 3D, polished, **and verification-visible** (golden shape functions, mass-conservation, deterministic P2G, per-material physical invariants). That intersection — a **multi-material MPM playground that proves each material's constitutive physics** — is the moat, still uncontested.

## 3. Algorithm & accuracy architecture

**The gated path is the faithful explicit MLS-MPM port; the materials are additive constitutive branches; the closed-form + per-material invariants are the moat.** All new materials are pure per-particle constitutive functions (return maps) applied before P2G — they do not alter the transfer, so the golden B-spline / partition-of-unity / mass-conservation moat is untouched.

### 3.1 Gated MLS-MPM core (ports the verified reference)
- **Shape function:** quadratic B-spline, 3-node stencil (27 in 3D), base `floor(pos/dx + 0.5) − 1`, weights `w0=0.5(1.5−fp)²`, `w1=0.75−(fp−1)²`, `w2=0.5(fp−0.5)²` — matching the golden table exactly (`shape_functions.py`; the single most-verified line).
- **Transfer:** APIC/MLS — P2G scatters `affine = stress + mass·C` (fused); grid update (momentum → velocity, gravity, boundary); G2P gathers velocity + affine C. Constant `(Dp)⁻¹ = 4` for the quadratic stencil.
- **Deformation & stress:** F update per particle; neo-Hookean/fixed-corotated stress (the verified core).
- **Precision:** **f32 particle/grid state**; **fixed-point integer atomics for P2G accumulation** (§ 3.3). Determinism posture: **device-scoped bit-exact** (run-twice) + **cross-device distributional** (observables) — cross-device bit-exactness NOT claimed (chaotic deformation + per-vendor rounding + SVD variance). The boids/SPH honest boundary.

### 3.2 Additive materials (each extends the verification moat; all reference-less — the invariant IS the verification)
- **Elastic jelly (have it — the only material with a committed reference):** neo-Hookean (log J), exactly `compute_particle_stresses` in `packages/mpm-multimaterial/mpm_multimaterial/reference/mls_mpm.py` — including its **`log_j = −30` guard when `J ≤ 0`**, which the WGSL port must reproduce verbatim (it is part of the verified behavior). Invariant: bounded elastic energy.
- **Snow (Stomakhin 2013, params paper-verified: θc=2.5e-2, θs=7.5e-3, ξ=10):** F = Fᴱ·Fᴾ, exp hardening `µ=µ₀e^{ξ(1−Jᴾ)}` (λ hardens identically), **SVD singular-value clamp of Fᴱ to [1−θc, 1+θs]**, **fixed-corotated** elastic energy — i.e. snow brings a *second* elastic model plus a 3×3 SVD, all new WGSL (v0.2 correction: NOT "near-zero cost"; the recipe is proven in Hu's upstream 88-liner but absent from our verified reference). New invariant: post-return-map elastic singular values always in range (a verifiable bound).
- **Sand (Klár 2016):** StVK-on-Hencky + closed-form Drucker-Prager return map in principal-stretch space, 3 cases. New invariants (v0.2, terms defined — the formula is Klár's verbatim): with **εp = trial Hencky strain** `ln Σ` and **Hp = projected Hencky strain**, Case III (cone-face projection) must satisfy **`tr(Hp) = tr(εp)`** — the non-associative flow's volume preservation, *scoped to Case III only* (Case II tip projection deliberately discards stress and allows volume gain); and **Case II returns Σ=I ⇒ zero Kirchhoff stress** — tension particles separate stress-free. Shares the SVD with snow.
- **Water (Tampubolon 2017 / Tait EOS — v0.2 recitation, see § 2.2):** track only `J=det(F)` as a per-particle scalar, pressure `p(J)` weakly-compressible; live-loop stiffness/γ tunable for the single-substep budget (Splash precedent: γ 5→1). New invariant: density↔volume relationship `J·ρ = ρ₀` to tolerance.

### 3.3 GPU engineering & the determinism subtlety (load-bearing)
- **P2G scatter via fixed-point integer atomics — mandatory, not optional.** WebGPU has **no float atomics at all**, so i32/u32 atomic accumulation is the only scatter mechanism — determinism comes free with it. (The determinism *motivation* still stands: f32 atomic-add, where it exists, is empirically non-deterministic — Defour & Collange, FedCSIS 2015, verified: 2¹⁶ values, condition number 10⁸, GTX680 — 1000/1000 runs distinct.) `encodeFixedPoint(x) = i32(x · M)`; integer add is associative ⇒ **order-independent ⇒ run-twice reproducible**. **Multiplier M (v0.2, converged):** every surveyed browser MPM uses **M = 1e7** (PB-MPM uniform default, webgpu-ocean `override`, holtsetio/flow) — adopt 1e7 as the starting point, then *measure* overflow headroom + mass-conservation at target N (an i32 saturates at ~2.1e9/M ≈ 214 mass-units/cell at 1e7; the per-cell accumulation bound, not global N, is what matters — validate on the worst-case preset).
- **Tiered scatter architecture (v0.2 — the optimization plan, informed by the survey):**
  - **Tier 1 (v1 baseline, ships first):** split passes with **plain global i32 atomics**, webgpu-ocean-style (clear → p2g → grid-update → g2p). Survey evidence says this alone sustains 6-figure N (Splash reaches ~1.6M water with no sort and no tiling). Simple, portable, byte-identical run-twice out of the box.
  - **Tier 2 (the measured upgrade, adopt if the step-2 benchmark misses targets):** PB-MPM's bukkit pipeline in 3D — counting-sort binning (`count → insert → allocate`), **indirect dispatch** per non-empty bukkit (empty space = zero dispatches; doubles as particle reordering for coherent stencil access), and **fused G2P2G** staging the grid tile in `var<workgroup>` atomic i32 (claymore's ~2× on the ~80%-of-substep transfer stage, already proven in WGSL by PB-MPM). Budget check: a 4³-cell bukkit + quadratic halo = 7³ vertices × 4 i32 ≈ 5.4 KB, × 2 (src+dst) ≈ 11 KB — fits the **16 KB `maxComputeWorkgroupStorageSize` floor** (do not design for 32 KB; real adapters reject it). Both tiers use the same encoding ⇒ same gate.
  - **Substeps are the biggest lever** ("cut substeps, not particles" — Splash ships 1 substep/frame by softening the water EOS). Live-loop dt/substep count adaptive; the canonical gate scene stays pinned at dt=1e-4.
  - **WebGPU limits to design against:** default **8 storage buffers per stage** (PB-MPM's g2p2g uses exactly 1 uniform + 8 storage; sph-water hit the same wall — its CSR trick is the precedent), 10 requestable at ~99% adapter coverage but treat as an upgrade, not a baseline. `timestamp-query` (shipped, 100 µs quantization) for the per-pass budget HUD.
- **THE SUBTLETY (drives § 2.1):** fixed-point atomics are bit-identical to a *fixed-point* reference, **NOT** automatically to the existing **f32 lex-order** CPU reference — quantization differs. So the web gate claims **device-scoped run-twice determinism + bit-identity to a fixed-point oracle**, and matches the CPU captures on **observables**, not per-particle f32 equality. (Making the browser bit-identical to the CPU reference would require re-expressing the CPU P2G in the same fixed-point encoding — a *backend* change, out of scope; flagged as a possible follow-up, § 10.)
- **Grid:** dense 3D grid (sparse/SPGrid rejected for WebGPU — § 9; the bukkit indirect dispatch recovers most of the sparse win). Adaptive-N live loop; the 1M capture is offline/CI. **Targets (v0.2, revised up from "lower than single-material" pessimism):** multi-material 3D **150–300K on a mid-range dGPU, 50–100K on iGPU** — WebGPUMPM did 200K multi-material in 2021 with zero optimizations, and the per-material cost is FLOPs + struct size, not an order of magnitude. Measure and document actuals at the step-2 checkpoint.

### 3.4 Aesthetic layer (never affects the gate — per-material rendering, upgraded v0.2)
- **The unifying trick (Splash, source-verified): one extra `p2gDensity` pass builds a density grid, and a single ray-march through it delivers shadows + self-shadowing for EVERY material at once** — water shadows, snow's soft self-occlusion, sand-pile shading — one pass, shared by all four materials, and it reuses the very P2G machinery the gate already proves. This is the single highest-leverage rendering addition for making a *multi-material* scene read as physical.
- **Water:** screen-space fluid rendering (the SPH pipeline — depth→thickness→smoothing→normals→Beer-Lambert), upgraded with the **narrow-range filter** (Truong & Yuksel 2018, Splash-proven in-browser) over the plain bilateral for cleaner silhouettes. **Snow:** soft instanced splats + per-particle sparkle noise + density-grid self-shadowing (survey: no browser demo has ever rendered snow properly — raw points was the 2021 state of the art — so this is cheap distinctiveness). **Jelly:** surfaced/specular blobs, subsurface-ish tint. **Sand:** instanced grains with per-grain hue jitter (holtsetio/flow proves instanced *meshes* with shadows at ~130K; impostor sprites as the fallback tier). **Per-material color** via the shared `common/common-web/src/colormap.ts`. All pure aesthetics, toggleable; a "raw particles colored by material/stress/speed" honesty view.

## 4. Scope — four layers

### 4.1 INTERACT
- **Material brush / dispenser** — pick snow/sand/jelly/water and drop/pour blobs into the domain; watch them interact in one solve (jelly bounces, snow packs, sand piles, water pools).
- **Drop / throw** — spawn a blob with initial velocity (the canonical drop-impact); **stir/push** — drag an impulse.
- **Movable floor / obstacles / tilt** — tilt gravity to trigger a sand avalanche or slosh the water; drop a rigid obstacle.
- **Sliders:** per-material params (Young's E, Poisson ν, snow hardening ξ + θc/θs, sand friction angle), particle count (adaptive cap), gravity, dt/substeps (live loop only; canonical pinned dt=1e-4).
- **Multitouch + pointer (v0.2).** The interaction bar for browser MPM is Grant Kot's Liquid Layers (multitouch, pen pressure); pointer-events with multi-pointer impulses cost little and read as magic on tablets — the drag/stir path should be written multi-pointer from day one.
- **Presets as data, not code (v0.2).** Every preset is a JSON entry in the data spine (§ 5): material blocks (AABB/sphere + material id + params + initial velocity), camera, render toggles. Canonical set: the gated **elastic drop-impact** (reproducible), **snow-pile / snowball toss**, **sand-hourglass / avalanche ramp** (tilt-triggered), **water-pour**, **jelly-stack drop**, **snow-globe shake** (impulse burst), and the **four-material showcase** ("dam of jelly, beach of sand, snow on top, water poured over"). Adding a template = one JSON entry; the current slider/scene state is **serializable to a URL query string** (share/bookmark a configuration — cheap, standalone-serve-safe, and turns visitors' experiments into templates).

### 4.2 EXPLAIN (equation → code legibility)
- The MPM pipeline (P2G → grid update → G2P → deformation/plasticity), the **quadratic B-spline** weights, the **APIC affine transfer** (`affine = stress + mass·C`), and each **constitutive model** (neo-Hookean; snow SVD-clamp + hardening; Drucker-Prager return map; det(F) fluid) — each next to the committed WGSL line and the NumPy reference it ports (`mls_mpm.py` / `shape_functions.py` anchors). Snippet anchors extracted at build time (§ 5), self-healing.
- **Why C1 continuity** (quadratic B-spline avoids cell-crossing instability), cited to the 2016 course.
- **The determinism story, honestly** — why float atomics are non-deterministic where they exist (Defour & Collange 2015: 1000/1000 distinct results), why WebGPU has *no* float atomics at all (making fixed-point i32 the only scatter mechanism — determinism as a free by-product of a hard constraint), and the device-scoped-vs-cross-device boundary + the fixed-point-vs-f32-reference subtlety. The honesty thesis made teachable (and unusually deep here).

### 4.3 PROVE (the moat — grows with the materials)
- **Live golden-value B-spline readout — flagship.** Evaluate the quadratic weights (and ∂) at the committed `mls-mpm-shape-functions.json` sample points on the visitor's GPU; display measured vs golden with the max deviation (~1e-16-class). Closed-form, exact, chaos-immune.
- **Partition-of-unity + mass-conservation P2G/G2P.** ∑w=1 live; total mass/momentum conserved across the transfer (fixed-point encoding shown not to leak mass).
- **Deterministic-P2G bit-identity.** Run the P2G scatter twice, SHA-256 the grid, show byte-identical — the fixed-point-atomics payoff (the MPM analog of boids brute≡sort / SPH hash≡brute).
- **Per-material physical invariants (new coverage).** Snow: elastic singular values stay in [1−θc, 1+θs] (plot the clamp). Sand: `tr(Hp)=tr(εp)` volume preservation + cone-tip stress-free separation. "Adding a material adds a proof."
- **Robust-observable match to the captures.** 5K capture on tight observables; 1M capture on chaos-tolerant aggregates (COM trajectory, KE, bulk height, per-material mass fractions) — per-particle explicitly not gated.
- **Verification card** bound to committed files (via § 5): gate kind `new_canonical`; golden-table sha; capture provenance (1M + 5K, seed 42, params, payload shas); reference determinism `bit-exact-same-hw`, browser `epsilon` device-scoped + the fixed-point-reference caveat.

### 4.4 RENDER (visually stunning, physics-honest)
- Per-material rendering (§ 3.4): screen-space water (narrow-range filter), splatted self-shadowed snow, surfaced jelly, instanced hue-jittered sand — all lit coherently by the **shared density-grid ray-marched shadow pass**, which is what makes four materials in one frame read as one physical scene rather than four overlaid demos. **Debug/honesty views** — raw particles colored by material / stress / speed / Jᴾ (plastic deformation), the grid overlay, the fixed-point-grid buffer, and a **per-pass timing HUD** (`timestamp-query`) so the perf story is inspectable, not asserted.
- **hiDPI** at `min(devicePixelRatio, 2)`. Poster + motion loop (`make-posters.mjs` / `make-loops.mjs`).

## 7. Governance & constraints

- **New WGSL kernel + additive materials, real `new_canonical` gate from day one.** Closed-form + determinism + per-material-invariant + robust-observable, not per-particle capture (chaos + fixed-point≠f32-reference). Not experimental-labeled.
- **HARD BOUNDARY — no backend changes.** No edits to `mls_mpm.py`, `shape_functions.py`, `invariants.py`, `sim.py`, the golden tables, the committed `captures/mpm-multimaterial-*`, or the sim spec-ref. The web build is additive. Re-expressing the CPU reference in fixed-point (to claim browser≡CPU bit-identity), or promoting new materials into the *verified* reference, are *separate* backend specs.
- **New materials are additive and self-verifying** — each ships with its own invariant (snow SV-bounds, sand volume-preservation); they do not touch the golden B-spline / transfer moat.
- **Cross-device bit-exactness NOT claimed** — device-scoped bit-exact + cross-device distributional; the fixed-point-vs-f32-reference subtlety stated in the demo (§ 4.2/§ 4.3).
- **Precision:** f32 state; **fixed-point integer atomics for P2G** (determinism + the bit-identity proof depend on it).

## 9. Out of scope / deferred / rejected-with-cause

- **Any backend change** — reference, invariants, golden tables, captures, spec-ref frozen for this work.
- **Porting EA PB-MPM's integrator — REJECTED (transfer mismatch).** PB-MPM is Position-Based (a per-particle constraint solve on deformation displacement, Jacobi-converged) and 2D; porting it would deviate from the verified explicit MLS-MPM core and forfeit the golden/observable bindings. Its unconditional stability (paper: dam-break stable at 30 Hz × 1 iteration where explicit needed 240 Hz) is bought with Jacobi softness — the wrong trade for a demo whose thesis is constitutive fidelity. Reuse only its *engineering* (fixed-point atomics, bukkit tiles + indirect dispatch, material-branch structure).
- **Per-particle `capture_roundtrip` gate — REJECTED (chaos + fixed-point≠f32-reference).** f32 can't match f64 per-particle over 500 steps, and fixed-point P2G isn't bit-identical to the f32 lex-order reference. Gate binds to closed-form + device-scoped-determinism + robust-observable (§ 2.1).
- **Two separate grids for multiphase — REJECTED (unnecessary).** The research **refuted 0-3** the "needs two grids" framing; one shared grid + per-particle constitutive models is correct for the showcase.
- **Multi-field contact / slip** — deferred (v2). Single-velocity-field MPM has documented "artificial sticking" (the no-slip constraint is inherent to a single-valued grid velocity field; multi-velocity-field contact traces to Bardenhagen et al. 2000/2001, graphics-grade version Han et al. 2019); acceptable for a visual showcase, a limit only for engineering-grade contact. **v0.2 note:** the cheap v2 fix is **ASFLIP (Fei et al. 2021)** — a particle *integration-scheme* change enabling easier separation, real-time-friendly, no second grid — evaluate it before reaching for multi-field contact (and do not mislabel it as a contact method).
- **Warp/subgroup-reduction P2G — still rejected, corrected grounds (v0.2).** The v0.1 claim "WGSL subgroups are experimental" is **stale — subgroups shipped stable in Chrome 134 (March 2025)**. The rejection stands on better grounds: subgroup sizes are opaque and non-uniform across vendors (8–64), reduction order is unspecified (**breaks the bit-determinism gate**), and Firefox/Safari coverage is unreliable. Fixed-point atomics stay the gated path; a subgroup fast path is at most an optional non-gated Chrome experiment.
- **CK-MPM compact kernel (arXiv 2412.10399) — REJECTED (moat conflict).** A C2 compact kernel shrinks the P2G stencil 3³→2³ and reports ~2× on transfer — but it *replaces the quadratic B-spline*, forfeiting the golden shape-function table, the single most-verified asset in this package. The whole demo thesis is that verification travels with the port; a faster unverifiable kernel is the wrong trade. Noted here so it isn't "rediscovered" later.
- **Sparse grid / SPGrid — REJECTED for WebGPU (not merely deferred, v0.2):** SPGrid-style virtual-memory tricks and persistent threads don't exist in WebGPU; the bukkit indirect-dispatch path (§ 3.3 Tier 2) recovers most of the empty-space win portably.
- **Cubic B-spline, rigid coupling, water+screen-space (if not v1)** — deferred (§ 5).
- **Portfolio-wide** extractor/dashboard/landing redesign; other demos; publishing (operator-dispatched).
