## 1. Purpose

Elevate the Growing-NCA demo from a *free-running canvas* into an *instrument* — and take a niche no NCA demo anywhere occupies. The § 2.2 survey is decisive and was re-confirmed adversarially (two independent agents, ~40 queries, could not refute): every NCA demo (Distill's WebGL demo, Hexells, cells2pixels, the Sakana/TF.js work) teaches *what* NCA grows; **none surfaces verification or determinism**, and Distill itself only *admits in prose* that its 8-bit-WebGL demo "differs slightly" from the f32 Python — never quantifying it, never offering a re-runnable check. This demo proves *that the learned computation is reproducible* and shows *exactly how — and how much — a trained model does or doesn't reproduce across implementations*, live on the visitor's own GPU.

It carries the richest honesty story in the rollout, told in two true scopes:
- **The most fragile gate holds — where the hardware agrees.** The web gate is `capture_roundtrip` **bit-exact 0/0** (`tools/productization/web-deploy/verify.py:348-374`) — any single ULP fails — and a *trained neural network run as a CA* clears it on the obtainable backends, because browser-Dawn and the wgpu-native canonical both compile to SPIR-V on the *same* RX 6800 XT RADV driver (`docs/_audits/phase-5/browser-divergence-resolution-landing-2026-06-09T13-24-25Z.md:68`, `:150`).
- **Bit-exactness is backend-conditional — and that is the point, not a caveat.** The audit is explicit: the bit-exact result "leaned on shared RADV"; on a distinct ALU (CI lavapipe, or a visitor's Metal/D3D12/NVIDIA backend) the *same trained weights* may round the conv reductions differently (`…resolution-landing…:150`, `:159`; `tools/productization/web-deploy/verify.py:1217-1225` `_gate_neural_ca_observable`). So the flagship (§ 3.3) does not *assert* zero — it **measures and displays** the visitor's actual `max_abs` vs the committed canonical: zero on a matching backend (a trained net, bit-identical), a tiny non-zero otherwise (f32 non-associativity — the same organism, different bits). This is precisely the phenomenon Distill noted and never measured.
- **The determinism mechanism is teachable.** The stochastic per-cell fire (Bernoulli 0.5) is a *stateless PCG hash* of `(x,y,step,seed)` matched to the NumPy oracle (`packages/neural-ca/typescript/src/nca_inference.wgsl:56-63`) — which is *why* a "random" learned update is bit-reproducible within a backend, and why the same-backend run-twice is byte-identical (§ 3.2).

### 2.2 Prior art & positioning (web survey, 2026-07-03; sources re-confirmed 2026-07-04)

Surveyed the NCA landscape — the foundational model, training regimes, the Distill/Google Self-Organising Systems lineage, and the interactive-demo canon. All architecture and follow-up citations below were re-checked against primary sources; the two corrections v0.1 needed are flagged.

- **Foundational model (CONFIRMED verbatim).** Mordvintsev, Randazzo, Niklasson, Levin, "Growing Neural Cellular Automata," *Distill* 2020, DOI 10.23915/distill.00023 ([distill.pub/2020/growing-ca](https://distill.pub/2020/growing-ca/)). 16-channel cells (RGBA + 12 hidden), fixed Sobel perception → 48-vec, ~8k-param 48→128→16 update MLP (zero-init final = residual do-nothing start), stochastic fire 0.5, alive mask α>0.1 via 3×3 max-pool. Matches this repo's kernel (`packages/neural-ca/typescript/src/nca_inference.wgsl`).
- **Training regimes & regeneration (CONFIRMED, with the nuance the damage brush needs).** Growing (overgrows/unstable), **Persistent** (pool-trained, stable — *this repo's shipped model*), **Regenerating** (pool + damage augmentation → robustly heals cuts), Rotating (steerable Sobel). Distill is explicit: persistence yields only *incidental partial* healing — "Patterns exhibit some regenerative properties upon being damaged, but not full re-growth" — while **robust regeneration requires the damage-augmented Regenerating regime**. So the shipped Persistent-disk checkpoint is expected to *partially* recover from damage, and the demo shows the α-mass curve rather than promising a full heal (§ 3.1, § 8).
- **Follow-ups (all CONFIRMED).** Self-classifying MNIST ([Distill](https://distill.pub/2020/selforg/mnist/)); Self-Organising Textures ([Distill](https://distill.pub/selforg/2021/textures/)); Growing 3D Artefacts ([arXiv:2103.08737](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.08737)); μNCA ([arXiv:2111.13545](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.13545)); attention-based ViTCA ([arXiv:2211.01233](https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.01233)); Isotropic NCA ([arXiv:2205.01681](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.01681)); Steerable NCA ([arXiv:2302.10197](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.10197)).
- **Demo lineage & GPU technique (re-confirmed firsthand).** Distill's demo is **hand-written WebGL/GLSL, 8-bit-quantized, 96×96, ping-pong framebuffers, perception+dense as SEPARATE passes** (not TF.js — the "TF.js playground" was an *unpublished internal tool*, one sentence in the article). Interactions: erase brush, double-click seed, speed slider, Growing/Persistent/Regenerating/Rotating model selector. Hexells ([znah.net/hexells](https://znah.net/hexells/)): WebGL, 8-bit PNG weights, hex-grid, damage brush + brush-radius/speed sliders + ~250 palettes (notably ships `seedrandom.js` **commented-out** — seeded determinism was considered and disabled). cells2pixels ([cells2pixels.github.io](https://cells2pixels.github.io/), arXiv:2506.22899, 2026): **WebGL2/SwissGL**, *fused* perception+MLP fragment shader, float textures, a **channel-visualization mode toggle** (rgb/albedo/normal/height/ao/roughness), and an **LPPN neural-field decoder** that renders a 128² lattice at up to 8192² without retraining. This repo's two-dispatch (update + mask) f32-storage-buffer design at 64² is *more numerically precise* than the 8-bit WebGL canon — which sharpens, not weakens, the verification story.
- **White space (positioning — the niche to own).**
  - (a) **No NCA demo surfaces verification/determinism** — none shows a trained model is run-to-run byte-identical, offers a re-runnable check, or diffs against a committed golden. (Nearest precedent is non-NCA: `webgpudna.com`'s live validation harness — the pattern to bring to NCA.)
  - (b) **None quantifies cross-implementation divergence.** Distill *admits* it in prose only: "The quantization was largely an afterthought… That's why there are slight differences in models' behaviours between the online demo and the Python version." We measure it, per visitor, live. Note our axis is subtler than Distill's: theirs is 8-bit-WebGL vs f32-Python (quantization); ours is **f32 vs f32 across GPU backends** (non-associativity) — full precision on both sides, and *still* divergent off the matching backend.
  - (c) **A learned model is a deterministic recurrent map run for many steps (Distill samples 64–96), so f32 non-associativity across backends yields bit-divergent trajectories** — motivating a bit-exact gate. **Corrected framing (v0.1 overstated this):** trained NCA are *not* chaotic; their attractors are Lyapunov-stable and perturbation-damping ([Kvalsund & Stovold 2026, arXiv:2604.12720](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.12720): "all the attractors are Lyapunov stable… small perturbations will always shrink"). So cross-backend f32 differences produce trajectories that are **bit-divergent but visually convergent** — the same organism, different bits — which is a *cleaner* statement of the two-scope thesis than "perturbations compound." (v0.1's `arXiv:2505.13058` citation is **removed**: that paper is "A Path to Universal Neural Cellular Automata" — universal computation, unrelated to FP sensitivity. The author-confirmed precision concern to cite instead is Distill's own: 8-bit quantization "not accounted for during training," mitigated with arctan compression "to preserve the highest accuracy around zero.")

### 3.1 Layer 1 — INTERACT (the template gallery + the regeneration brush)

The demo has **no cursor and no presets today**; this layer adds the de-facto NCA affordance set (confirmed as the canon across Distill/Hexells/cells2pixels: brush, seed, brush-size, speed, mode selector, play/pause/reset). All are live-loop writes to the live state buffer (`cur`) or live uniforms; the capture path `reset()`s to the seed and re-runs the pinned protocol (`packages/neural-ca/web/src/main.ts:182-221`) — gate-safe by § 6.

- **Damage / erase brush (the recognizable feature) — framed as a MEASURED experiment, not a promise.** Cursor zeros a disk of cells (write zeros into `cur` via `queue.writeBuffer`, the pattern `reset` already uses) → watch the organism respond. This checkpoint is **Persistent, not damage-trained Regenerating** (§ 2.1, § 2.2), so the honest, expected outcome is *partial* recovery — and the demo *shows* it: an **α-mass recovery curve** (reuse the existing `alphaMass` measurement, `packages/neural-ca/web/src/main.ts:247-249`) plotting mass vs steps-since-damage. Caption cites Distill's "some regenerative properties… but not full re-growth" — the brush teaches *why* the Regenerating regime exists.
- **Seed placement:** double-click (or a Seed mode) to add a seed cell (center-cell state, `seedState` pattern, `:40-47`) → multiple organisms interacting.
- **Mode selector (Brush / Seed / Drag)** — cells2pixels' cleanest UX; a single control disambiguates pointer intent. **Brush-size** control (S/M/L or radius slider).
- **Live fire-rate slider** (uniform `fire_rate`, written at `packages/neural-ca/web/src/main.ts:100`, currently the const `FIRE_RATE=0.5`) — asynchrony robustness; live-loop only (the capture/gate re-runs at the pinned 0.5).
- **Step controls:** play/pause/single-step/restart + a **steps-per-frame speed slider** (the current `STEPS_PER_FRAME=2` becomes live); a "grow → freeze → damage → measure" guided-experiment strip.
- **Template gallery** (definitions in the § 4 data spine, captioned with § 2.2 sources), realizable over the single committed checkpoint with runtime toggles:
  - *grow-from-seed* — single center seed → disk.
  - *persistent hold* — run to 1000+; the Persistent regime's defining property (perf-ledger:49 notes Growing would overgrow by ~step 200 — a contrast worth captioning).
  - *damage & measure* — the brush experiment + α-mass curve.
  - *multi-seed* — several seeds → interacting organisms.
  - *fire-rate sweep* — asynchrony robustness (live-only).
  - *hidden-channel tour* — cycle the 12 latent channels (a RENDER template, § 3.4).

### 3.3 Layer 3 — PROVE (verification bound to real data) — the flagship

Structure it exactly as the landed rd2d panel (`verify-panel.ts`): **(1) a data-bound card**, **(2) the live proof on THIS device**, **(3) the divergence post-mortem**. Every value comes from the generated data spine (§ 4) — no retyped constants.

- **Flagship — the backend-conditional live re-run (this is the headline).** Re-run the canonical seed-42 rollout on the visitor's GPU into scratch buffers (never the live state; the `stepOnce`/`readState` path exists, `packages/neural-ca/web/src/main.ts:123-154`), then **score all 21 captured `rgba` frames against the committed canonical using the gate's own criterion (`max_abs`, bit-exact threshold 0)** — a true client-side mirror of the CI gate's full-sweep comparison (`tools/productization/web-deploy/verify.py:355-360` stacks every frame), on the visitor's hardware. Crucially, it **displays what it measures, and does not assert zero**:
  - `max_abs == 0` → "a trained neural network, reproduced **bit-identical** on your GPU — your backend shares the canonical's reduction order."
  - `max_abs > 0` (expected tiny) → "**same weights, a visually identical organism, but not bit-identical** — your backend rounds the conv reductions differently. This is the f32 non-associativity Distill only noted in prose, measured on *your* GPU."
  - Show a **backend-identity line** — vendor / architecture / description from the WebGPU adapter info (read display-only via a fresh `navigator.gpu.requestAdapter()`, no change to the shared `createContext`), plus whether it is in the known bit-exact (RADV) family. This is what makes the two outcomes legible rather than looking like a bug. The rd2d panel already ships this honesty verbatim ("OVER the declared budget on your GPU — displayed, not hidden: you may be looking at a backend family this repo has not yet measured"; `packages/reaction-diffusion-2d/web/src/verify-panel.ts:300-301`).
- **Divergence overlay + step scrubber (novel, from § 2.2 white space):** a per-cell `|Δ|` heatmap between the browser rollout and the committed canonical, **with a step scrubber across all 21 captured frames (step 0, 50, …, 1000)** so the visitor can watch *where and when* a divergent backend first departs from the golden — computed by a small **on-GPU diff pass** (§ 3.5) and rendered in the single overlay fragment pass (§ 3.4). On a matching backend every frame renders **all zeros** — a striking live proof that even a trained f32 model is bit-identical here; off it, the scrubber shows the rounding first appear (typically deep in the rollout) and its spatial structure. Nobody in the NCA lineage shows either.
- **Run-twice proof:** re-run the rollout twice into scratch buffers, SHA-256 each `rgba` sequence via `crypto.subtle`, show the two digests are **identical to each other** (byte-identical replay on this device). Present this as *run-twice determinism*, distinct from the committed payload sha `078be967…` (which is the `.h5` file hash, § 2.1) — do not imply the browser digest equals it.
  - **FACT (constraint):** `crypto.subtle` needs a secure context; localhost (validate harness) + https (Pages) qualify. Plain-language notice if unavailable (rd2d pattern, `packages/reaction-diffusion-2d/web/src/verify-panel.ts:175-181`).
- **Verification card** data-bound (§ 4): gate `capture_roundtrip` bit-exact; banked RADV `max_abs` 0.0; run-twice byte-identical; determinism (within-WGSL bit-exact-same-hw / cross-stack epsilon; the backend-conditional note); cross-stack LPIPS 0.0316; provenance (seed 42, 64², 16ch, 1000 steps @ 50, payload `sha256:078be967…` labeled as the `.h5` file hash). Preserves and data-binds the already-exemplary honesty note (`packages/neural-ca/web/src/main.ts:277-289`); wires the § 2.1 checksum.
- **The honesty arc (post-mortem, rd2d-sibling):** the alarming pre-fix "0.72 divergence" (`docs/perf-ledger.md:86`) was the frontend capture/live-RAF **harness race**, **refuted** and fixed across all 7 demos (shared `runCaptureExclusive` lock; the `isCapturing()` guard visible at `packages/neural-ca/web/src/main.ts:307`); post-fix the most fragile gate holds bit-exact 0.0 on the obtainable backends (`…resolution-landing…:103`). Measured-then-declared: the scary hypothesis was killed by measurement, tolerance byte-unchanged. **Keep this separate from the residual backend-conditional caveat** (the flagship) — one is a *refuted* race, the other is a *live, honest* cross-implementation axis.
- **Training vs inference determinism panel:** training non-deterministic-by-design (EFECT bound 0.07 cross-seed), inference bit-exact same-stack-same-hw (`docs/sim-specs/continuous-ca/neural-ca/spec-ref.md:167-193`) — two honest postures for one learned model.
- **Falsifiability probe ("make it fail"):** perturb the fire rate or the seed and re-run → the browser frames no longer match the canonical (`max_abs` jumps to O(0.1+); pinned at fire 0.5, seed 42) → a visible FAIL across the scrubber, so PASS reads as a real test result.

### 3.4 Layer 4 — RENDER (presentation quality)

The current display is premultiplied RGBA over white (`packages/neural-ca/web/src/render.wgsl:28-30`). All additions render as **branches of one fullscreen fragment pass** keyed by a `mode`/`channel` uniform (§ 3.5) — no extra compute passes — and are gate-safe (§ 6):

- **Hidden-channel visualization (unique + stunning):** false-color the 12 hidden channels via the shared colormap module (`common/common-web/src/colormap.ts`) — the invisible "chemical" state driving growth, shown for the first time. The hidden channels are unbounded (`docs/sim-specs/continuous-ca/neural-ca/spec-ref.md:68`), so squash before mapping (an **arctan** compression, echoing Distill's own near-zero-precision choice) with running per-channel scale. Two modes: a per-channel selector and a fixed-basis (or PCA-style) false-color of all 12; the cells2pixels `visMode` toggle is the closest prior art.
- **Divergence + alive overlays:** the § 3.3 `|Δ|` heatmap; an alive-mask (α>0.1) overlay; a **tiled multi-panel** mode (`floor(uv·N)` in one pass) to show several channels/overlays at once — the "many effects on screen" ask, at one draw call.
- **Crisp "fat pixels":** the render already indexes the storage buffer directly (`packages/neural-ca/web/src/render.wgsl:20-23`), which is inherently nearest/blocky — keep it; snap the canvas backing store to a multiple of 64 under `devicePixelRatio` for square cells on hiDPI. Raw-grid toggle.
- **Principle:** every visual element is a real channel of the state — no decoration that misrepresents the computation.
- **Poster + optional growth loop:** the static poster exists (`tools/productization/web-deploy/web/pages/assets/neural-ca.png`, the disk); regenerate if the render changes (`make-posters.mjs`) and consider a seed→organism growth loop (`make-loops.mjs`) — operator decision (§ 9).

### 3.5 Layer 5 — OPTIMIZE (headroom for "many effects at once")

Grounded in a WebGPU-technique sweep against the actual code. **Governance:** all of this applies to the **live loop and the client-side verify re-run** (presentation/instrument); the **capture path numerics stay byte-for-byte identical** and the changes must preserve run-twice byte-identity and the bit-exact gate (re-validate per § 7). WebGPU exposes a single `device.queue`, so the levers are batching and time-slicing, not a second queue.

- **Do the verification diff ON the GPU.** Replace full 256 KB state readbacks (`readState`, `packages/neural-ca/web/src/main.ts:144-154`) in the compare path with a reduction compute pass emitting a single `max_abs` (and per-cell `|Δ|` for the overlay) into a tiny buffer; read back only those bytes. This is what makes a *live* divergence overlay + live `max_abs` affordable.
- **One encoder, one submit per frame.** `stepOnce` currently creates two encoders and submits twice per step (`packages/neural-ca/web/src/main.ts:126-140`) → with `STEPS_PER_FRAME=2` that's 4 submits/frame plus the render. Batch `update`→`mask`→render into one encoder + one `queue.submit` (implicit hazard sync covers the producer→consumer buffer dependency). **Verify the results are byte-identical after this change** (they should be — only scheduling changes).
- **Cache bind groups.** `frame()` rebuilds the render bind group every frame and `bind()` allocates two per step. With the 3-buffer rotation there is a small fixed set — pre-create and index by parity.
- **Chunk the live re-run** across `requestAnimationFrame` (e.g. 20–50 steps/submit, the rd2d `CHUNK=500` pattern, `packages/reaction-diffusion-2d/web/src/verify-panel.ts:202-213`) so a 1000-step rollout never freezes the view; acknowledge in-UI that a heavy re-run briefly shares the one queue.
- **Keep** storage buffers (16-channel arbitrary layout is buffer-shaped) and `@workgroup_size(8,8)` (tiles 64² exactly, hits the 64-lane sweet spot).
- **Optional:** feature-gated `timestamp-query` GPU timing (per-pass ms, rolling average, degrade to CPU-frame ms when unavailable) — a "measured performance" element that fits the repo's ethos.
- **Rejected optimizations** (§ 8): f16 state (voids bit-exactness), 16×16 workgroups (premature at 64²), MRT for overlays (a mode-uniform single pass is simpler/cheaper).
