## 1. Lattice geometry

The D2Q9 discrete velocity set has 9 directions. The numbering
convention used by
`tools/testkit/golden/tables/lattice/d2q9-equilibrium.json` and by the
`packages/lbm-multiphase/lbm_multiphase/reference.py` NORMATIVE kernel is

| Index | Vector | Speed magnitude |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | $(0, 0)$ | 0 |
| 1 | $(+1, 0)$ | 1 |
| 2 | $(0, +1)$ | 1 |
| 3 | $(-1, 0)$ | 1 |
| 4 | $(0, -1)$ | 1 |
| 5 | $(+1, +1)$ | $\sqrt 2$ |
| 6 | $(-1, +1)$ | $\sqrt 2$ |
| 7 | $(-1, -1)$ | $\sqrt 2$ |
| 8 | $(+1, -1)$ | $\sqrt 2$ |

Opposites (bounce-back pairs): $\bar 0 = 0$, $\bar 1 = 3$, $\bar 2 = 4$,
$\bar 5 = 7$, $\bar 6 = 8$.

## 2. Weights and lattice sound speed

Write $w_0$ for the rest weight, $w_a$ for the 4 axis weights, $w_d$
for the 4 diagonal weights. Requiring the discrete moments to match the
Maxwell–Boltzmann moments up to fourth order (isotropy conditions):

- normalization: $w_0 + 4 w_a + 4 w_d = 1$
- second moment: $\sum_i w_i c_{i\alpha} c_{i\beta} = c_s^2\,\delta_{\alpha\beta}$
  gives $2 w_a + 2 w_d = c_s^2$ per axis;
- fourth moment isotropy
  ($\sum_i w_i c_{i\alpha} c_{i\beta} c_{i\gamma} c_{i\delta} =
  c_s^4(\delta_{\alpha\beta}\delta_{\gamma\delta} +
  \delta_{\alpha\gamma}\delta_{\beta\delta} +
  \delta_{\alpha\delta}\delta_{\beta\gamma})$):
  the $xxxx$ component gives $2 w_a + 2 w_d = 3 c_s^4$ and the $xxyy$
  component gives $2 w_d = c_s^4$.

Solving: $c_s^2 = 1/3$, $w_d = 1/36$, $w_a = 1/9$, $w_0 = 4/9$ —
the Qian 1992 D2Q9 constants:

$$w_0 = \tfrac49,\qquad w_{1..4} = \tfrac19,\qquad w_{5..8} = \tfrac1{36},\qquad c_s^2 = \tfrac13 .$$

## 3. Equilibrium distribution

Second-order Hermite expansion of the Maxwellian (Qian 1992 eq. 5):

$$
f_i^{\mathrm{eq}}(\rho, \mathbf{u}) = w_i \rho \left[ 1
  + \frac{\mathbf{c}_i \cdot \mathbf{u}}{c_s^2}
  + \frac{(\mathbf{c}_i \cdot \mathbf{u})^2}{2 c_s^4}
  - \frac{\mathbf{u}^2}{2 c_s^2} \right].
$$

Moments (exact identities, re-asserted per table row):
$\sum_i f_i^{\mathrm{eq}} = \rho$,
$\sum_i f_i^{\mathrm{eq}} \mathbf{c}_i = \rho\mathbf{u}$,
$\sum_i f_i^{\mathrm{eq}} c_{i\alpha} c_{i\beta} =
\rho u_\alpha u_\beta + \rho c_s^2 \delta_{\alpha\beta}$.

**Shifted (DDF) form** — the f32-conditioning storage contract
(Skordos lineage; Lehmann et al., Phys. Rev. E 106, 015308 (2022)):
store $\bar f_i = f_i - w_i$ (equilibrium at rest, $\rho_0 = 1$), so

$$
\bar f_i^{\mathrm{eq}} = w_i \left[ (\rho - 1)
  + \rho \left( s_i + \tfrac12 s_i^2 - \tfrac32 \mathbf{u}^2 \right) \right],
\qquad s_i \equiv 3\, (\mathbf{c}_i \cdot \mathbf{u}),
$$

which is the pinned operation order shared by
`packages/lbm-multiphase/lbm_multiphase/reference.py` (`run_scene`) and
`packages/lbm-multiphase/web/src/lbm_core.wgsl` (`collide_stream`).

## 4. Viscosity

Chapman–Enskog at second order (Qian 1992 § 3; Krüger 2017 ch. 4):
$\nu = c_s^2 (\tau - \tfrac12)$ in lattice units — the family staple
shared with the D3Q19 sibling.
