# Tolerance-budget amendment — NEW category `lbm-quantized` (C-1 U-3)

**Operator approval:** D-2, ratified at the C-1 charter ratification
(`docs/phases/phase-6/c1-charter.md` § 10) — a NEW category for the
frontier-moment-encoded LBM's bounded-quantization equivalence, with bounds
**measured-then-declared at build**.

**Measurement (stage 1c, canonical horizon):** the quantized Stack-C trajectory
(`poiseuille-64x32-seed42-step1000`, full-horizon stability-guided ranges, margin 0.25,
16-bit codes) vs the landed numpy parent capture, all 1001 frames, fields `rho` + `u`:

| Field | max_abs | note |
|---|---|---|
| rho | 8.55e-15 | the conserved moments quantize on a narrow range — effectively exact |
| u | 3.12e-6 | u_peak 8.65e-3 → peak-relative 3.6e-4; max_rel over \|u\|>1e-6 cells 1.98e-2 |

**Declared (with documented margin):** `absolute = 1.0e-5` (3.2× over the measured
max_abs), `relative = 5.0e-2` (2.5× over the measured small-cell max_rel); comparison
form `err ≤ absolute + relative·|parent|` per field. NOT a widening of the parent `lbm`
category (1e-5), which continues to govern true cross-stack ports — the U-3 **f64 mode**
measured INSIDE `lbm` at rho 3.7e-15 / u 1.6e-15 over 201 frames.

**Stage-1b defect honestly recorded:** a 64-step calibration envelope clamped the
still-accelerating momentum moments (u error ~60% of peak); ranges now span the full
trajectory horizon (the paper's "stability-guided" requirement, measured the hard way).
