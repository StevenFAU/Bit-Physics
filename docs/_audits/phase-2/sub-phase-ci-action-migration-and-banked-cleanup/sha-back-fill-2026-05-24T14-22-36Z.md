## § 2. Back-fill-induced sha-drift (audit-chain-correctness § 9 N2 banked precedent)

sha256 CHANGE between their first commit (`c3fa95c` / `a0afe82`) and this back-fill commit.
Per the N2 precedent, any downstream artifact citing those sha256s must cite the
**post-back-fill HEAD value** (verify via `git show <this-commit>:<path> | sha256sum`, do NOT
transcribe). The plan-drafting landing audit deliberately recorded `evidence_hashes` ONLY for
the stable doc anchors (conventions / architecture / methodology) to avoid this drift; the
probe + charter are listed there under `evidence_paths` (existence-check) without hashes.
