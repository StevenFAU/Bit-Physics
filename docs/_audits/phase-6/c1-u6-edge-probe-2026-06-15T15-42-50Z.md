## § 3 — Method core (from the anchor, read at probe this session)

Grid-based flow-map advection WITHOUT storing the per-step velocity-buffer history that
BiMocq/PFM-style long maps require:

1. **Gradient Evolution** — the flow map's **first-order** spatial derivatives (the
   Jacobian ∇ψ) are *evolved* directly on the grid alongside the map, giving accurate
   gradients without reconstructing them from a stored velocity buffer.
2. **Tetrahedron-based Epsilon-Difference** — **higher-order** derivatives (the map
   Hessian / curvature terms needed for accurate transport) are computed by a finite
   ε-difference over a tetrahedral stencil of nearby evolved samples — the
   memory-reduction lever (no buffer retained).
3. **Hermite interpolation** — map value + evolved gradients feed a (C¹) Hermite
   interpolant for high-accuracy sampling at departure points.
4. **O(1) memory, independent of flow-map length** — the headline, *mechanically
   measurable* claim (charter § 3.6: "a rare rigorous frontier claim"): paper reports up
   to **90 % backward-map memory reduction** (37.89 GB → 8.54–10.79 GB). Vorticity
   preservation comparable to buffer-based methods; demonstrated on vortical flows + a
   dye-drift test.
