### 4.1 — Sim A: 3dgs-mpm-sh-update — golden tables

**G1 — SH-rotation numerical golden `c' = D(R)·c` (Prong 1; ≥3 anchors).**
- **A1 (closed-form, degree-1 Wigner-D == rotation):** the real degree-1 SH band transforms
  under a rotation `R` exactly as `R` acts on the direction components. In the renderer's basis
  (`common/common-3dgs/src/common_3dgs/render.py:87`: `−C1·y·sh[1] + C1·z·sh[2] − C1·x·sh[3]`, i.e. the degree-1 coefficients pair
  with the signed direction components `(y, z, x)`), the degree-1 block of `D(R)` IS `R`
  expressed in that signed `(y,z,x)` basis — a **closed-form 3×3** for any chosen `R` (e.g.
  `R_z(90°)`). **Source:** real-SH rotation — Green, R. (2003), "Spherical Harmonic Lighting:
  The Gritty Details," GDC; cross-checked against the Inria real-SH basis constants already in
  `common/common-3dgs/src/common_3dgs/render.py:34`. *Stage-0: web-re-verify the real degree-1 Wigner-D / basis-sign convention
  against the renderer's exact `_eval_sh` form — do NOT assume the convention.*
- **A2 (rotation-equivariance oracle — IMPLEMENTATION-INDEPENDENT, the primary gate):**
  for arbitrary coefficients `c`, rotation `R`, and query direction `d`:
  `eval_SH(D(R)·c, R·d) == eval_SH(c, d)` to machine precision. This is the **defining
  equivariance** of the SO(3) action on SH spaces and does NOT depend on the specific Wigner-D
  closed form → it independently validates the rotation for ANY degree. **Source:** SH
  rotation-equivariance (group-representation property; verify numerically against the landed
  `_eval_sh`). *This is the strongest, most independent anchor — it catches a wrong Wigner-D.*
- **A3 (degenerate / pure-stretch — fully independent):** polar-decompose `F = R S`; for a
  **pure stretch** (`R = I`) the SH rotation is the identity → `c' == c` (recovers the landed
  MVP "SH frozen" as the `R=I` special case → a consistency anchor against task-8). And a
  **pure rotation** `F = R` (`S = I`) rotates SH by `R` with covariance unchanged in shape.
