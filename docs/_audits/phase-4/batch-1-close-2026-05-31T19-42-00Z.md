### Sim 2 — lenia-diff

  `dG/dσ = 16·base³·(u−μ)²/(9σ³)` (smooth interior, `base = 1−(u−μ)²/(9σ²) > 0`; autodiff ==

## §8 — Sim-4 D-WARP-ADJOINT probe outcome (BLOCK gate)

**CONFIRMED DIFFERENTIABLE — NO BLOCK** (`tools/testkit/probes/reports/eulerian-smoke-diff.md` §1).
The gap a naive port would hit: the landed reference's primitives are NumPy-marshalling wrappers
(`wp.from_numpy(...)` → `out.numpy()`), and the `.numpy()` round-trip **severs the `wp.Tape`**. The
diff variant therefore re-implements the smoke step's two load-bearing primitives — the bilinear
semi-Lagrangian backtrace gather and the explicit diffusion — as **on-device `requires_grad`
`@wp.kernel`s** recorded inside a single `wp.Tape` (it does NOT wrap the reference). Probe (Warp CPU
f64): autodiff `∂Loss/∂u₀` == the **exact** analytic linear operator `2 Mᵀ(M u₀ − t)` to rel
**1.1e-16**; == central FD to **3.1e-9**; `∂Loss/∂ν` == analytic to **0.0**. The integer base-node
`wp.int32(xb)` is non-differentiable (zero-gradient cell selector — correct) while the bilinear
weights carry the gradient → the adjoint is the exact transpose `Mᵀ` within a cell (gradient golden
scoped to constant velocity → globally-linear M → no cell-boundary kink).
