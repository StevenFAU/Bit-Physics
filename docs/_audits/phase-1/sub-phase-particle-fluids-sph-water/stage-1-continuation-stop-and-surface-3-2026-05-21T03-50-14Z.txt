--- Root cause analysis ---

The R17 surface's expected wall-clock estimate of 10²-10³ s assumed
cKDTree-based neighbor query would dominate per-step time at ~1s and
that vectorized density_evolution would add maybe ~1-2s. Per-step
total expected ~3-5s. 1000 steps × 5s = 5000s = 83 min. Estimate was
incorrect.

Actual per-step cost (inferred from RSS oscillation timing and the
217-min total wall-clock):

  1. pair_lists_from_positions (cKDTree.query_pairs at N=1M with
     cutoff=0.024 producing ~25M unique pairs + symmetrize +
     lexsort): ~5-8 seconds.
  2. density_evolution_vectorized (vectorized math on 50M
     directed pairs — broadcasting (M, 3) arrays for r_ij, v_rel,
     grad; aggregate intermediates ~7 GB at peak): ~5-8 seconds.
  3. Total per step: ~10-15 seconds.

Projected total: 1000 steps × 13s = 13,000 s = 217 min — matches
observed elapsed.

Plus post-loop work (_trajectory_to_step_states with 11 frames ×
density_vectorized + StepState construction + h5 write): ~60-90s
additional.

Final projected wall-clock: ~225-250 min (~3.75-4.25 hours).

The pure-Python NumPy SPH simulation at 1M particles fundamentally
processes 25-50M pair operations per step × 1000 steps × FP math
overhead in interpreted NumPy. Even with C-implemented cKDTree and
vectorized inner math, the sheer scale of data movement and
intermediate array allocation produces a ~hour-scale wall-clock.

--- What the R17 routing got right and what it missed ---

R17 correctly identified that the pure-Python cell-list outer loop
was the immediate bottleneck and that scipy + cKDTree could fix it.
The fix WAS effective for the immediate symptom: without cKDTree
we'd be at 14+ hours; with cKDTree + pair-arrays we're at ~4 hours.

But the 10²-10³ s expected wall-clock was an order-of-magnitude
optimistic. Pure-Python NumPy at 1M-particle scale produces ~10⁴ s
even with best-in-class scipy spatial primitives, because the inner
vectorized math is moving 1-2 GB of intermediate arrays per step.

--- Four remediation paths (R-class addendum — call it R18) ---

(α) **Add numba as an sph-water dependency; JIT-compile the
    density_evolution_vectorized inner math.** Numba lowers the
    vectorized loop to native machine code without the 50M-pair
    intermediate array allocations (each tensor operation becomes
    a fused inner loop). Expected wall-clock improvement: 10-30×
    over current pair-array approach. Projected total: 10-30 min.

    Pros: keeps the canonical descriptor at full 1M scale;
    incremental optimization; numba is mainstream and well-supported.
    Cons: adds numba (~200 MB installed) + LLVM transitive footprint
    + compilation overhead on first call (~5-10s) + determinism
    requires fastmath=False discipline.

(β) **Downsample canonical N.** Use a smaller-N descriptor (e.g.,
    dam-break-100K-particles-seed42-step1000 — 100K particles ×
    1000 steps). At N=100K the per-step cost drops by ~10× (5M pairs
    instead of 50M); total 1000s × 1.3s/step = 1300s = ~22 min.

    Capture size: 100K × 56 B × 11 frames = ~59 MB (within original
    64 MB ceiling).

    Pros: minimal change; pure-Python NumPy at smaller scale is
    tractable; bypasses the entire R12-R18 sequence; the 1 GB
    ceiling raise (W1) can stay as future-proofing.
    Cons: requires Appendix D § D.2.3 amendment (Phase-1-retroactive)
    OR per-sub-phase descriptor override (documented gap between
    Appendix-D claim and committed capture).

(γ) **Accept the long runtime as the canonical baseline.** Let the
    capture run to completion (~225-250 min total). Perf-ledger
    records the actual wall-clock. The descriptor matches Appendix D
    verbatim; the Stack-C C++ implementation in Phase-2+ produces
    the same capture at proper performance.

    Pros: no further code changes; Appendix D fidelity preserved;
    documents the Python-NumPy-reference's actual operating envelope

    Cons: 4-hour single-shot wall-clock is uncomfortable for
    iteration; if any Stage-2 work needs to regenerate, that's
    another 4 hours; the perf-ledger row records a number that's
    not informative for cross-stack comparison.

(δ) **Defer the canonical capture to Phase-2+ Stack-C.** Land
