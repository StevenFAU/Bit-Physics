--- Verdict: R12 STOP-AND-SURFACE triggered ---

The canonical descriptor `dam-break-1M-particles-seed42-step1000`
cannot be produced at sensible multi-frame capture cadence without
exceeding the 64 MB pre-commit ceiling raised at RD-3D Stage 1 N4.
Even the most aggressive cadence (final-frame-only, 2-frame H5)
produces a ~107 MB capture, almost 2× the ceiling. Only a single-
frame (final state, no IC) capture fits, marginally, at ~56 MB
payload + ~3 MB H5 overhead = ~59 MB total.

--- Three remediation paths (per sub-phase plan § 9 R12 / Item 5) ---

(a) **Raise .pre-commit-config.yaml maxkb ceiling** (e.g., 256 MB or
    larger). Lets the canonical capture ship as-is at a sensible
    multi-frame cadence. Trade-off: future captures of similar order
    (MPM/LBM/eulerian-smoke 3D) inherit the higher ceiling; CI
    storage cost rises proportionally. Consistent with RD-3D Stage 1
    N4 precedent (10 MB → 64 MB raise to absorb the RD-3D 46-MB H5);
    the precedent supports periodic ceiling lifts when new sims
    demand it. Default-likely choice for this sub-phase to land
    cleanly.

(b) **Downsample the canonical descriptor's capture cadence**
    (capture_interval=1000 + no IC → 1 final-frame only; or even
    fewer per-particle fields per frame, e.g., position-only at
    24 B/particle → 24 MB payload). Trade-off: per-frame fidelity
    is reduced; the descriptor's name still claims `step1000` but
    the H5 only contains the final state. Acceptable per spec
    Appendix D § D.2.3 implicit semantics (the descriptor names the
    sim configuration, not the per-frame capture cadence); the
    capture-writer cadence is a per-sub-phase implementation
    decision documented in the manifest.

(c) **Amend Appendix D § D.2.3 for a smaller canonical N**
    (e.g., 100k or 200k particles instead of 1M). Trade-off: a
    Phase-1-retroactive amendment; touches an append-only-protected
    document; requires a separate Phase-1-amendment sub-phase OR
    operator override. Most invasive; least preferred.

--- Recommendation (advisory; operator routes) ---

Reasoning:
  - Consistent with RD-3D Stage 1 N4 precedent: when a new sim's
    canonical capture exceeds the current ceiling, the ceiling is

    capture_interval=100 → 11 frames → ~620 MB total). Note: 256 MB

    protected.
